How this works

Oracle text is always scored using IDF-weighted overlap — rare terms like "storm" count more than common terms.

Strictness controls how picky each factor is. Strict means "cards must be very close to match" — a mana cost off by 2 gets almost no credit. Loose means "roughly similar is good enough" — being off by 2 still counts. Think of it like a focus knob, not a volume knob.

MTG filter removes ubiquitous terms (creature, target, player, graveyard, battlefield…) from oracle text matching. Two removal spells won't score higher just because they both say "target creature."
